About CengBooch

At CengBooch, we brew small-batch, home-made kombucha that’s as spunky, fizzy, and fearless as you are.

Born from a love of cozy chaos and creative flavors, CengBooch is more than just a drink—it’s a vibe. Our brews are packed with flavor, probiotics, and a little bit of magic, all served in a cozy, cottage-inspired shop that feels like home.

Fun. Fizzy. Fearlessly fighting bacteria. That’s the CengBooch way.
